Reward for each move

Change the points and see what the agent learns to value.

Step cost applies to every action, including a knock. The goal bonus is added once, on the move that gets the second supporter to vote. The optional priority turf scheme adds its bonus on every action that starts at a supporter’s house, even after that supporter has voted. Standard is selected by default. Changing a reward setting resets the agent so it learns under the selected points.
